# SWDB3001 — Database operation failed

## Meaning

A bounded SQLite operation or optional PostgreSQL operation/startup
qualification failed.

## Trigger

The persistence dependency rejects, times out, or cannot complete the requested
operation or PostgreSQL preflight check.

## Correction or recovery

Preserve the local authority boundary, apply the owning retry policy, and
inspect the persistence health evidence.

## Operational effect

The affected durable operation is not accepted merely because a caller waited.

## Rationale

Persistence failures require explicit evidence and must not become silent state
loss.

## Alternatives or migration

Continue only through a documented local fallback that preserves authority.

## Related diagnostics

- `SWDB8001` reports ambiguous durable evidence requiring reconciliation.
