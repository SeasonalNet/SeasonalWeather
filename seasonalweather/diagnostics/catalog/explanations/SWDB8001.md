# SWDB8001 — Database recovery requires reconciliation

## Meaning

Durable persistence evidence is incomplete or ambiguous.

## Trigger

Restart or recovery finds unfinished, conflicting, or unacknowledged durable
state.

## Correction or recovery

Run the owning bounded reconciliation path and retain its evidence before
resuming the affected authority.

## Operational effect

The ambiguous record is not treated as committed or discarded by inference.

## Rationale

Durable state must be proven by its authority, not by one file or memory view.

## Alternatives or migration

Keep the service in its documented safe mode until reconciliation completes.

## Related diagnostics

- `SWDB3001` reports the underlying persistence operation failure.
